Mount Carmel is now accepting applications for the 2027–28 academic year, from LKG to Class 12.

## How to apply

1. Call the school office on 04651 287 977 to check whether seats are available in your child's class.
2. Visit the campus to meet the principal and collect the application form.
3. Return the completed form with the documents requested by the office.

Parents of children joining Class 11 should ask about the subject groups offered this year before applying.

You can also [send an enquiry online](/admissions#enquire) and the office will call you back.
