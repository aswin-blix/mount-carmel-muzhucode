Weekly sessions in the Atal Tinkering Lab have started for the 2026–27 academic year.

Students in the upper primary and secondary classes are working in small teams. The first projects use sensors and simple circuits to solve everyday problems around the school, such as reminding students to switch off lights and fans.

The best projects will be shown at the school science exhibition later in the year.
