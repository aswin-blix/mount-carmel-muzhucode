date: 2026-08-15

summary: Students and staff marked India's 80th Independence Day with flag hoisting, songs and speeches.

---

The school community gathered on the playground on 15 August to celebrate Independence Day.

The national flag was hoisted, followed by the national anthem. Students from different classes presented patriotic songs, a skit on the freedom struggle and short speeches in English and Tamil.

Sweets were distributed to all students at the end of the programme.
